Dungeons and Dragons has this long stnading problem where having skill proficiencies doesn't really mean anything if you have bad luck, which i think is dumb and stupid and also dumb. A +4 to arcane is quite nice, yes, but what happens if I roll a 4 and the DC is 15? Either the DM plays by the rules and supposedly my elf wizard just happens to have a convenient gap in his knowledge about this particular subject, or the DM doesn't and I get the knowledge anyway and my roll just doesn't mean anything. kinda sucks, tbh.



so. what's the point in rolling? why not just automatically succeed at what you're proficient in?



in spaceships and starmaps, which was a kinda-osr dnd-sci-fi clone or whatever the hell else kinda labels we want to tack on there, you just had two skills that you auto-succeeded at.

"but acrisius" you may say, "what if my soldier" (fighter equivalent) "has a proficiency in fighting? do they just continuously win every fight because they keep hitting?"



"you recalcitrant dromedary" i say "the soldier doesn't have a proficiency in fighting. the soldier has a proficiency in military history and weapons knowledge" 



this, i have found, drastically reduces the times where i as a GM have to say "okay well you've been rolling poorly this whole time so i'll toss you this freebie" and "okay well we have to play by the rules so you just don't know this i guess" while still providing enough events that players don't feel like they just don't need dice to roll



i had a somewhat similar "evening out" procedure in the acrisian apocrypha (which i don't think i've put out yet) where everytime you rolled a one (it's a d6 system, so not too rare) you'd gain a point of luck that you could spend 1-to-1 as an additive bonus to other checks, though I suppose that was easier to implement because of smaller die sizes. with DnD that uses a d20, you'd have to say everytime you failed a check by, say, more than 3 or 5, you'd get a point to gain a +2  or +3 bonus to a roll and maybe a natural 1 would net you a +4 or something but that's... that's all math and grinds the game to a halt anytime someone wants to add more than two types of points to their roll and whatnot. 



but i digress.
