The men of Gizaad are split into twelve tribes, as was ordained by the Basnich'Twen, All-Seeing Eye, for it feared for its life if all twelve had been united. Even the weakest of creatures can build gallows to hang gods if they wished.

YARIJ
The men of the forest, branch-climber, axe-user. They have a tail long and thin, strong enough to carry themselves and prehensile enough to write with. Their toes are clawed and long to better hang to the branches they climb upon. In their longhouses, the wisest amongst them is treated the leader.

ZIKARI
Desert dwellers. A thick pad of fat on their back or chest swells with water to live through the long, hot summers of the Ijikar Flats. Without the wood needed to create doors, they instead utilize the Doorstone, placed in the doorway when the sun sinks to keep the heat in and the bugs out.

KAMALORI
In their swamps, their houses are held aloft by massive platforms with enormous pillars that sink into the mud. They secrete a fine mucus when it's needed, and sweat toxins. They are famed for their woodworking-- in no other place other than the Fen can bog oak exist.

BAALHOR
The underground people. They dig their massive and ornate tunnels for nearly their whole lives. With specialized forearm muscles and fingers, it's no great effort. A tapetum lucidum allows them to see in their subterranean homes.

VIRAN
Tall and slender, their digitigrade legs and specialized hearing allow them to hear incoming invaders and run fast away-- or charge straight into them. A precious few have been granted the gift of horns.

MAREUS
Lurking down near the reeds of the banks of the Great Delta, one might catch the sight of a predatorial slit-pupil eye staring from just beneath the water, skin coated in scales mouth ringed in sharp teeth. A hunting people, strength and speed are respected.

VOSAIG
Hewing their houses from the great sides of the Forathi, the ancestor volcano, the Vosaig grow bones and ribs outside their skin as well as inside to tolerate the scorching heat of their home.

GAZEN
Islanders, they are blessed with the drinking of saltwater and a rich green skin with which to photosynthesize, making the most of the Sun. In the hotter months, some may sprout flowers.

AJIRI
Island nomads, sailing across the seas a dozen times-- no one knows the currents like they do. An enlarged chest allows for larger lungs, and webbing between their fingers allows them to outpace any other tribe in the water.

KSITO
Floating on the wind below Forathi live the Ksito, the massive platform their houses sit on floating on the air vents that blast up from below. Massive gliding fins decorate their backs, letting them fall nearly sideways.

ICHREN
Friend to all but a home, the Ichren are nomads. They can shapeshift, and often do so as simple expressions of their emotions. Those tribes that host the Ichren may suddenly find that they resemble them much more with each passing day.

CAZO
Standing tall and stout, covered in white fur, the Cazo live far north and far south in the cold taigas of Gizaad, among the pine and larch. In such a cruel environment, those among them that tell the best stories are treated the leaders.
